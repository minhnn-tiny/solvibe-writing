\section*{Data Availability}
The development counts come from archived run records. The qualification counts come from dated screening, build and assessment records. No anonymous replication package has yet been deposited. We plan to release the screening decisions, versioned grading rules, aggregate results, analysis, and an account of omissions and known exposure. The public package will identify upstream materials and include copies where permissions allow. Until the study is complete, access to withheld assessments and assignment keys must be restricted to preserve blind grading. Any items still restricted at submission will be identified together with their release conditions.
