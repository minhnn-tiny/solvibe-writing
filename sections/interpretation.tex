\section{Validity, interpretation and research use of AI}
\paragraph{Selection and dependence.}
Public, fixed, buildable findings with preserved ordinary-use evidence form a selective sample of Solidity maintenance. The need for a pinned historical service can select by platform even before a patch is attempted. Requiring two alternative examples and a third hidden obligation selects again. The family cap limits the influence of one upstream codebase; it cannot make shared dependencies or closely related fixes independent. We will publish losses by stage, family, reference route and reason, and interpret the randomized effect only for the eligible paired cases.

\paragraph{What the grader can establish.}
An agent may implement the behaviour exercised by the report while overlooking one that the project never measured. Reference revisions provide two historical anchors for a verdict but cannot settle every new patch. The stronger outcome measures the particular second obligation that the evidence supports. It confers no claim of complete security. Labels that depend on a specific error message, a refactor bundled with the historical fix, or code copied between tests need human scrutiny. Work on vulnerability repair has likewise examined patches manually and identified risks to functionality in smart-contract fixes~\cite{bui2024apr4vul,bobadilla2026fixes}. The plan calls for two unconflicted human auditors for a sample of 32 submitted patches, with report assignment, system identity and automatic scores concealed. Their time and independent participation must be confirmed.

\paragraph{Who read the evidence.}
Codex assisted with the study design, interpretation of prior runs, organization of screening records and preparation of this manuscript. Claude Code assisted with engineering and with preparing and reading case material. A Claude Code reader assessed case provenance, ordinary use and proposed behavioural distinctions. Other model-assisted or custodian readings compared the candidate obligations with the factual report and reference change. Historical follow-up assessments on Ekubo were informed by model review. These activities can create correlated judgments, particularly when tools from the same family participate in preparation and repair. The human admission reader checks specific claims and evidence versions. Only initial judgments genuinely made before exposure count in an independent agreement calculation. A human final decision after seeing model advice measures assisted review. Machine recommendations and human admissions must remain distinguishable in any released table.

In the last 48 scope reviews the reader recorded eleven instances in which code was consulted or both judgments drafted before the first reading was saved. It also reported incidental exposure to demonstration material in eleven records. These events weaken claims about the independence of those readings. They do not create human votes. We will report any missing independent readings, later overrides and evidence corrections. The list of reviewed claims remains tied to the version the reader actually saw.

\paragraph{Exposure and time.}
The audit finding and its eventual repair may already have been public during training. A hidden assessment in our study is hidden from the assigned session; it does not demonstrate lack of prior model exposure. Sessions on previously studied repositories receive a separate analysis. Model upgrades, tool changes and shared service interruptions can also align successes or failures across blocks. Interleaved assignment, version recording and transparent reporting of interrupted starts limit what must be guessed afterwards.

\section{Conclusion}
The development records identify two distinct maintenance decisions. Passing the reported example can leave another check failing, and even a battery that passed on both security and ordinary use can miss a caller-visible defect. Qualification supplies a further result: compiling the historical revisions and obtaining evidence for affected ordinary use are separate obstacles. We have specified a primary endpoint that remains meaningful for a single reported obligation and a separate estimate for cases that support a second one. Determining whether the example in the ticket changes the agent's repair still requires the signed cases and randomized sessions described here.
